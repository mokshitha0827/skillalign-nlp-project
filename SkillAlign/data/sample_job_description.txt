Junior NLP Engineer

About the role
We are looking for a Junior NLP Engineer to help build text-understanding features for our learning platform. You will work with data scientists and engineers to turn research ideas into reliable features.

Requirements
- Strong Python programming skills.
- Hands-on experience with natural language processing, including text classification.
- Experience with scikit-learn and Pandas for data preparation and modelling.
- Familiarity with transformer models such as BERT and the Hugging Face ecosystem.
- Working knowledge of PostgreSQL and SQL.
- Experience building and consuming REST APIs.
- Version control with Git and collaborative code review.
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to work in a team.

Nice to have
- Experience deploying applications with Docker.
- Exposure to AWS or another cloud platform.
- Knowledge of Kubernetes.
- Experience creating dashboards or data visualization.

About us
We are a friendly, fast-growing company that values curiosity and learning. We use Terraform and Kubernetes internally.
